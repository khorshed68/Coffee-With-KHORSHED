Our Story

A cup, a chair
& a conversation.

Coffee With KHORSHED started with a simple idea: the best talks happen over a really good cup. So we source carefully, roast in small batches and take our time with every drink.

Whether you need a quick espresso on the way to work or a slow afternoon with a pour over and a friend, there is always a seat waiting for you here.
